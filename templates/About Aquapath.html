AquaPath AI

How the AI works

AquaPath turns a pair of selected ports into clear route comparisons for a safer, more informed shipping decision.

🌐

01 · Flask web connection

Flask serves the website and receives the selected start and end ports through a route-calculation API request.

🧭

02 · Pathfinding

Python uses depth-first search to discover connected shipping paths, then keeps the three shortest candidates for evaluation.

🌊

03 · Weather and traffic

Live wind and wave information is combined with local CSV traffic data to evaluate conditions along each candidate route.

🧠

04 · Risk scoring

The scoring model weighs weather risk at 65% and traffic risk at 35%, producing a warning level from 0.00 to 5.00.

🗺️

05 · JavaScript route map

JavaScript displays the returned results and draws port points and route paths on a world-map image using a canvas overlay.

🏆

06 · Three ranked options

The site presents the three evaluated routes with their scores and highlights the strongest route first.
